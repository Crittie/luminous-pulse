Data Partnerships

Second, get paid for the data your business already produces.

AI labs now license anonymized operational data to train their systems: the SOPs, CRM records, project histories, and workflows your team creates every day. I work with several AI data buyers as an independent referral partner. I introduce qualified companies to the buyer that fits their data best, and I help owners protect their interests in the process.

Published payouts to companies range from $100K to $2M or more per approved data package, depending on the buyer and the data, always quoted after a short evaluation.
  • You decide what is in scope. Privileged, HR, payroll, health, and customer-confidential data stay out.
  • Scope is agreed in writing before anything is shared.
  • The right buyer for your data. Different buyers value different data, so I match your company with the one that fits best.
